05 Questions

The questions everyone asks.

Can we see the products you've built — live?+
Yes. On a call we'll walk you through the real platforms — Bridge and the Bhisi app, live, not screenshots. Fifteen minutes is enough to judge the level of finish you can expect for your own project.
What does a project cost?+
It depends on scope — but you'll never have to guess. The scoping call is free, and every number is agreed in writing before we start. A website or a dashboard is a straightforward one-time build; a full platform that we host, run and support is scoped just as clearly. No hourly meter, no surprises.
How long will it take?+
Websites: typically a few weeks. Custom platforms and mobile apps: built in phases, with a working version you can click from week two and something usable in your hands as early as possible. Speed comes from the same small team designing and building — no hand-offs.
Is our data safe — and is it ours?+
Your data is always yours. It lives in a professionally managed database with backups, access control and an audit trail — and you can export it whenever you like. We take care of the platform itself: hosting, updates and security are on us, so you never need your own tech team.
Can you take over or fix an existing system?+
Yes. We audit what you have, tell you honestly whether it's worth repairing or rebuilding, and we integrate with the tools you already use rather than forcing replacements.
What can AI automation actually do for us?+
Anything your team repeats on a screen: reading and answering routine messages, creating and chasing tasks, moving data between tools, drafting documents, flagging exceptions. We build agents around your real workflow — and you decide exactly what they're allowed to do on their own.
What does the cyber security service include?+
A practical audit of your apps, sites and infrastructure; penetration testing; fixes and hardening; and a clear report your management can actually read. And everything we build for you ships secured by default — access control, encryption, backups, audit logs.
Do you only work with companies in the UAE?+
We're licensed and based in Dubai — and we work remotely with clients anywhere. Calls on your timezone, updates on WhatsApp or email, wherever you are.
What happens after launch?+
We don't disappear — we're the ones running it. Hosting, monitoring, backups and updates are handled by us, support is a WhatsApp message away, and when you're ready for phase two, the team that built it is already there.
